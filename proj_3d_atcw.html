A Town Called Woods



3D • Render • Blender

A Town Called Woods is about a seemingly ordinary but mysterious town surrounded by forests in the middle of nowhere. From the surface, it looks like just another average, peaceful town, but there is always something lurking in the shadows...

Inspired by Simon Stålenhag's work, these renders aim to capture the mundaneness of realism and the fantasy of surreal strcutures.
